Who We Are

At MYSARA Handicrafts Store, we are the result of collaboration among passionate artisans who share the vision of expanding Malaysia’s traditional culture while introducing the heritage of other countries to our community. Our store is not only a place to shop for authentic handicrafts, but also a hub for cultural exchange — giving tourists a deeper appreciation of Malaysia and offering locals new knowledge through diverse workshops. Visitors can join short crafting sessions or enroll in structured courses that provide valuable skills for work opportunities. Beyond culture and learning, we dedicate part of our sales to support NGO programs, ensuring that our growth also uplifts underprivileged communities. Together, we preserve tradition, share knowledge, and create impact.

Mission

Our mission is to preserve and promote traditional handicrafts by collaborating with skilled artisans, offering affordable handmade products, and creating opportunities for learning through workshops and courses. We aim to empower communities by channeling part of our sales to support NGOs and programs that help those in need.

Vision

Our vision is to become a leading cultural hub that connects Malaysia’s rich heritage with global traditions, inspiring both locals and tourists to appreciate, learn, and carry forward the art of handicrafts. We aspire to build a sustainable future where culture, creativity, and community support go hand in hand.
