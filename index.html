WHY CHOOSE US?

we are dedicated to preserving tradition, empowering artisans, and giving back to the community.
With a team of over ten skilled experts specializing in batik, weaving, and wood carving, we ensure every piece reflects authentic craftsmanship and cultural heritage.
We are actively contributing to NGO programs that support underprivileged communities and provide aid to those in need.
Choosing us means supporting artistry, sustainability, and social responsibility all in one place.

ABOUT OUR STORE



MYSARA Handicrafts Store, we are proud to offer a wide range of handmade products including batik bags, woven baskets, and wooden crafts created by our team of more than ten skilled artisans.Beyond selling unique and authentic items, we also provide engaging workshops where customers can learn traditional crafting techniques. Our mission is to make quality handicrafts affordable and accessible, while preserving cultural heritage. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted destination for craft lovers, and we actively contribute to NGO programs that support underprivileged communities, ensuring that every purchase also helps those in need.
